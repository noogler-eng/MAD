AI Engineering Handbook

Ye handbook AI Engineering ke liye hai — model banana/training karna (ML) is scope mein nahi hai. Focus hai: pehle se trained foundation models (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama...) ko production products mein use karna — agents banana, RAG/MCP se context dena, tool calling, system design, aur scale/latency ke real trade-offs.

📌 ML vs AI Engineering — line kahan khींchi hai

ML Engineer model architecture design karta hai, data pe train karta hai, research karta hai (weights.bin banata hai). AI Engineer already-trained foundation model ko leta hai aur usse ek real product banata hai — prompting, RAG, tool calling, agents, aur woh saara infra jo LLM ko app mein reliably, fast, aur affordably chalata hai. Ye poora handbook isi dusre role ke around hai.
