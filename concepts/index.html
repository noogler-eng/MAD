Backend Engineering from First Principles

Ye handbook ek 23-video playlist ke upar bana hai, lekin transcript ka dump nahi hai — har concept ko root level tak khola gaya hai: kya problem thi, us problem ka solution kaise evolve hua, aur aaj production mein us solution ka actual shape kya hai. Har chapter mein architecture diagrams (Excalidraw-style, inline SVG) hain jo woh cheez dikhate hain jo video mein sirf boli gayi hai.

📌 "First principles" ka yahan matlab kya hai

Framework-first seekhne mein tum @app.get("/users") likhna seekh lete ho, par pata nahi hota ki us decorator ke peeche TCP socket, HTTP parsing, route matching, aur serialization ka poora pipeline chal raha hai. Is handbook ka rule ulta hai — pehle layer ka mechanism, phir framework ka syntax. Isliye har chapter "problem kya thi" se shuru hota hai, "library kaunsi use karein" se nahi.

🧠 Kaise use karo ise

Pehle playlist ka video dekho (1x pe, bina notes) — speaker ka reasoning aur order samajhne ke liye. Uske baad ye chapter padho — jo skip hua tha wo yahan expand kiya gaya hai, aur diagram se poora model ek saath dikh jaata hai. Har chapter ke top pe uske source video ka link hai.
